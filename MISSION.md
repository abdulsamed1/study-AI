# Mission: Become a hireable Frontier Deployed Engineer (FDE)

*Draft 2026-10-09. Confirm or edit in the first session; the mentor must not change it without asking.*

## Why

Get hired as an FDE by being able to show one real, evaluated, secured, deployed, and handed-over agentic system, and by being able to defend every design decision in it. The role standard is in `AGENTS.md` section 2.

## Success looks like

- I can take an ambiguous business problem, decide whether AI is the right tool (and say "no" when it is not), and justify the level of autonomy.
- I have a repository for one capstone system with tests, an evaluation set (including adversarial cases), tracing, a security review, a deployment, and a runbook.
- I can produce and defend these artifacts: Architecture Decision Records, a Threat Model, an Evaluation Report, a Runbook.
- I can diagnose a production incident from evidence (using the performance map) and explain what I have and have not done, truthfully, in English.

## Constraints

- Daily learning happens in a ChatGPT Project and NotebookLM; agentic only occasionally. I do not edit files by hand: in ChatGPT/NotebookLM, progress lives in the Project's own memory (see `AGENTS.md` section 3).
- Explanations in simple; technical terms, code, and interview answers in English.

## Out of scope

- Training foundation models; ML research and advanced math (basic statistics for evaluation is in scope).
